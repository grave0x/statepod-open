# Naming Rationale

**Public command:** `sw`  
**Public project:** SwarmState  

We considered `ss` for internal use due to its brevity. It was rejected for public adoption because of historical and contextual sensitivities, and a conflict with the Linux socket statistics tool.

`sw` was chosen because:

- It is short and fast to type.
- It suggests both **swarm** and **software**.
- It has no negative historical association.
- It is broadly unambiguous across professional and community settings.
- It aligns with the calm, private, and trustworthy image of the project.

Internal aliases may exist for developer convenience but must never appear in public documentation, marketing, or customer-facing demos.
